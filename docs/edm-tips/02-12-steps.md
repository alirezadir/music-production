# 🎧 12 Steps to Succeed in the Music Industry (For Bedroom Producers)

**YouTube Video**: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ErrkYfrf0HA](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ErrkYfrf0HA)  
**Creator**: EDM Tips (Will)

## 1. What’s Your FIRE Goal?
- Set a **clear, exciting dream** (e.g., world tour, album release, producing full-time).
- Make it **specific** and write it down — revisit it regularly.
- Ask *why* it matters to you — emotional motivation fuels long-term action.

## 2. Commitment
- **Total commitment** means finding a way through every obstacle.
- Passion is a mix of **love** (for your vision) and **hate** (for regret or wasted potential).
- Successful producers commit deeply — especially when life gets hard.

## 3. Choose Your Genre
- Pick **one genre** to focus your energy and improve faster.
- Don’t chase trends — choose the genre you truly enjoy listening to and creating.
- Look at your own playlists for guidance — let taste lead, not hype.

## 4. Music First
- Don’t prioritize social media or branding early.
- Focus on becoming **really good first** — aim to be 90% as good as your favorite artists.
- Learn by **reverse engineering** reference tracks instead of guessing. Every great artist started here.
- Avoid plugin overwhelm — **master stock plugins** before buying more.

## 5. SMART Sub-Goals
- Break your FIRE goal into smaller, achievable chunks using the **SMART** formula:
  - **S**pecific  
  - **M**easurable  
  - **A**chievable  
  - **R**elevant  
  - **T**ime-bound  
- Example: “Release one track a month for the next 12 months.”

## 6. Process-Based Goals
- Focus on what you control: practice, learning, producing — not external validation.
- Good examples:
  - "Produce music 5–10 hours per week."
  - "Finish one full track per month."
- **Consistency > outcome obsession**.

## 7. Regular Action
- Block out dedicated weekly time for music — even with a job or family.
- 5–10 hours/week consistently over 3–10 years can yield professional results.
- Progress is about **showing up**, not waiting for inspiration.

## 8. Self-Analysis, Improvement & Organisation
- After every track, ask: “Where did I fall short compared to pro references?”
- Improve your weakest links (e.g., sound design, harmony, mix).
- Organize your samples, presets, templates — streamline your workflow.

## 9. Mentorship & Feedback
- Don’t go it alone — feedback and mentorship save years of trial and error.
- Choose mentors with real-world results in your genre.
- Feedback should come from people who understand your music style.

## 10. Community & Networking
- All music opportunities come from **relationships**.
- Be helpful in communities: remix others, attend events, support artists.
- Offer value without expecting something back — it always returns.

## 11. Don’t Take Things Personally
- Separate yourself from your work — critique is not an attack.
- Be open to **constructive feedback**, and learn to ignore haters.
- Get feedback from **relevant listeners** — not just friends or family.

## 12. Show Up & Never Give Up
- **Persistence is the ultimate differentiator**.
- Every successful producer simply didn’t quit — they evolved.
- 1 year isn’t enough. **3 to 10 years of focused effort** is a realistic path to success.

## BONUS: Understand the Industry
- Learn how the business works: labels, A&Rs, publishers, sync licensing.
- Study how to get your music **signed, placed, and paid**.
- Be an artist **and** an entrepreneur.
